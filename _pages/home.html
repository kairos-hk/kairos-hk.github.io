Welcome to my homepage

Dongyoung Kim
Undergraduate Researcher · Founder & CEO

I am an undergraduate at KAIST and the founder and CEO of PatchOne Industries Inc.

My research focuses on cybersecurity and AI, with interests in AI-agent privacy, digital forensics, side-channel security, and post-quantum cryptography for embedded systems.

I work across research, product development, and technology policy, building practical security systems and exploring their applications in aerospace.

At PatchOne, I develop AI-driven security assessment and compliance automation. I also conduct R&D and AI policy research at the Ministry of Science and ICT, Republic of Korea.

More about me · LinkedIn · GitHub · ORCID

Research interests

  • AI security & privacy: user-intent leakage through agent discovery metadata and continuous security assessment.
  • Digital forensics: volatile-memory evidence, portable operating systems, and wearable devices.
  • Embedded cryptography: post-quantum secure communications on resource-constrained CubeSat platforms.
  • Side-channel security: information leakage, countermeasures, and policy implications.
